AIORA-Toolkit Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

AIORA-Toolkit Beta is a browser extension that captures a full-page screenshot and an offline copy of the current page, and downloads both straight to your computer. Everything it does happens on your own device. There is no account, no server, no stored state, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

What this extension does not do

  • It does not have a server, a backend, or any form of analytics.
  • It does not sell, share, or transmit any data to any third party.
  • It does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal registration.
  • It does not run in the background on pages you haven't asked it to act on.
  • It does not keep a library, history, or any record of what you've captured — nothing persists between uses.

What data it touches, and where it stays

The extension holds nothing. Each click reads the current page once, builds the screenshot and HTML files entirely in memory, and hands them straight to your browser's own Downloads folder — nothing is written anywhere else, and nothing is remembered once the download finishes.

Permissions this extension requests

PermissionWhat it's for
activeTab / scriptingAct on the page you're currently viewing, only after you click the toolbar icon.
downloadsSave the screenshot and HTML files you create to your Downloads folder.
Host access (all sites)Lets the save-page feature fetch images/CSS/fonts from the current page's own origin and other domains it loads resources from, so the saved copy looks right.

Your control over your data

  • Every file it creates goes straight to your own Downloads folder — manage, move, or delete it the same way as any other downloaded file.
  • Uninstalling the extension leaves nothing behind, since it never stored anything in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continuing to use the extension after an update means you accept the current version.
